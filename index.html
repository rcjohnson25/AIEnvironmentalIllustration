How much water does your AI usage take up?

Choose a model, a typical response, and how often you use it. The visualization translates the estimate into familiar everyday water use.

How is this estimated?

Values use the supplied EcoLogits-derived model/scenario estimates as fixed baselines. The calculator multiplies the selected per-request estimate by requests per day and days. These are modeled estimates, not direct measurements of an individual request; real water use varies with hardware, cooling, datacenter conditions and workload.
